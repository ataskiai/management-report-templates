# Management report templates for small companies

Free Excel and Word templates for monthly finance reporting. They contain no macros and need no signup.

| File | What it is | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| `13-week-cash-flow-forecast-template.xlsx` | 13 weekly columns with live formulas: opening cash, receipts, disbursements (payroll, suppliers, rent, loan, tax, owner draws), net flow and closing cash. Weeks that fall below your minimum cash level are flagged. Includes an Instructions sheet and a worked Example sheet with illustrative figures. | [13-week cash flow forecast](https://ataski.com/guides/13-week-cash-flow-forecast) |
| `board-pack-template.docx` | Monthly board pack sections with guidance in each: headline summary, KPIs, P&L vs budget with commentary, balance sheet, cash and the 13-week outlook, risks, and decisions needed. | [Board pack template](https://ataski.com/guides/board-pack-template) |
| `monthly-management-report-template.xlsx` | P&L vs prior month and vs budget, with variance and % formulas. Also a balance sheet comparison, a KPI sheet (gross margin, operating margin, DSO, current ratio) and a commentary sheet. | [Monthly management report template](https://ataski.com/guides/monthly-management-report-template) |

To see what a finished report looks like, open the sample monthly management report for a fictional company: https://ataski.com/sample/financial-analyst/reports/management-report

For benchmarks to compare your ratios against, see [small company financial benchmarks](https://github.com/ataskiai/small-company-financial-benchmarks), the median ratios of ~700 small US public companies.
